I am a third-year student double majoring in Urban Technology and Environment at the University of Michigan. I grew up in Shenzhen, China, which sparked my interest in how cities interact with people and the environment.

This site is coursework for UT330, Interaction Design and Urban Experiences, taught by Ron Bronson at Taubman College.

How this site is built

Plain HTML, no build step, published with GitHub Pages. The styling comes from simple.css, a stylesheet that styles ordinary HTML without needing any classes, plus my own custom.css on top of it.
