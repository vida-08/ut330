Past 2 weeks: Wayfinding Walk in South State Street

September 11, 2026 · Week 2

Class Content

At the start of our course, after going through the course logistics, our professor Ron introduces an activity where we sketch out different websites built for the prompt, one is for an cafe (which I named it as "Friend's Cafe"), another is for an aquatic center who has a bunch of stuff going on.

My takeaway from this activity is that, the most important priority when building website, is to identify the hierarchy of the information, and to make sure the most important information is easy to find. I also learned that, when designing a website, it is important to consider the user experience, and to make sure the website is easy to navigate.

Assignment 1 · Field Study: Spatial Wayfinding & Digital IxD

The first assignment is to do a field study on spatial wayfinding and digital interaction design. We are asked to go find a space on or off campus, and take 3 pictures following the prompts, and do a brief presentation of our findings.

I choose the South State street, the buzzling commercial street where Target is located. The first picture I took is actually the Constraints & Feedback (the content going onto the 3rd slide). I picked that shot because its a good specimen of different forms of constraints designed for different users.

My second shot is the paint door on the door at Rally House.

My third shot is the intersection with East Washington St.
